Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

The short version

  • Whimzyy is a personal productivity site. There are no ads and we never sell your data.
  • Without an account, everything stays in your own browser.
  • With an account or guest profile, your Whimzyy data is saved to our cloud database so it follows you across devices.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Whimzyy ("we", "us") is an independent project run by Amruzinggg. You can reach us at YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

WhatWhy

We do not collect your location, contacts, payment details, or the content of the pages you visit.

Where your data is stored

  • In your browser (local storage): all Whimzyy settings and progress, whether or not you have an account. You can clear it any time in your browser settings.
  • In our cloud database (provided by Supabase): the same data, only if you continue as a guest or log in. Each person can only read and change their own rows.
  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages serves the site files.
  • Third-party files: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a code library from the jsDelivr network. These providers may see your IP address when your browser fetches them.

How we use your data

Only to run Whimzyy: to sign you in, save and sync your data, and keep the site working and secure. We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Guest profiles

If you choose "Continue as guest", we create an anonymous profile with a random name. It has no email attached. If you log out of guest mode without saving it to an email, that guest data can no longer be reached, so please save it first if you want to keep it.

Sharing

We share data only with the services that run Whimzyy on our behalf (Supabase for login and storage, Netlify for hosting), and only as needed to provide the site. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account or guest profile exists. If you ask us to delete it, we remove your account and the data tied to it. Backups held by our providers may take a short time to clear.

Your choices and rights

  • See or export your data: email us and we will send you a copy.
  • Delete your data: email us from the address on your account, and we will delete your account and everything saved to it.
  • Log out: logging out removes Whimzyy data from that device (your cloud copy stays until you ask us to delete it).
  • Clear local data: you can clear your browser's site data at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example India, the EU or the UK), you may have additional legal rights over your personal data, such as correction or objecting to processing. Contact us and we will help.

Cookies and storage

Whimzyy uses your browser's local storage and a login session to remember your settings and keep you signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Security

Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the database is set up so people can only reach their own rows. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Honest note: your saved data is protected by login and database rules, but it is not yet end-to-end encrypted. This means the person who runs the Whimzyy database could technically read what is stored in it.

Planned: end-to-end encryption

We are working towards end-to-end encryption, so your journal and other saved data would be locked with a key only you hold (a recovery key you keep safe). Once that is live, nobody else, including us, could read it. This is planned for the future and is not available yet. We will update this page when it is.

Children

Whimzyy is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For larger changes we will also tell you on the site.
