Plug in the extension 🔌

Websites can't peek at your other tabs (privacy, bestie), so a tiny Chrome extension does the blocking and this page is its remote. One-time setup: 1. unzip whimzyy-detox-extension   2. open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode   3. hit Load unpacked, pick the folder   4. refresh this page.

Still not working? This page lives at . On chrome://extensions check that Whimzyy Detox is switched on, press its reload ⟳ button, then refresh this page. The extension only talks to the address listed in its manifest.json.
